About The Table

A restaurant built around the simple idea of coming together.

Restaurant atmosphere photography

01 — Our Story

The best meals rarely end when the plates are cleared.

The Table began with a simple belief: restaurants are about more than what arrives from the kitchen.

They are places where birthdays stretch into evenings, friends become regulars and conversations last longer than planned.

We created The Table to make room for exactly that: thoughtful cooking, generous hospitality and the kind of evening nobody is in a hurry to leave.

Dining room and guests
Table detail

02 — Our Kitchen

Rooted here. Inspired everywhere.

South African ingredients are the starting point of our kitchen, not its boundary. Our menu follows the seasons, combining familiar flavours with contemporary technique and influences gathered from further afield.

We cook with restraint. Good ingredients should still taste like themselves.

03 — The Kitchen

Cooking with a sense of place.

Chef Naledi Mokoena

Executive Chef

Naledi's cooking begins with the produce, flavours and memories that feel close to home, then gives them room to move in a contemporary direction.

Her approach favours clarity over complication: thoughtful technique, careful seasoning and plates that still feel generous enough to share.
